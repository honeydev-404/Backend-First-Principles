
What is Authentication and Authorization :-
* Authentication -> “Who are you?”
    * It is the process of checking if we are the person that we are pretending to be or not.
* Authorization -> “What can you Do?”
    * It is the process which takes place after authentication because first we have to be identified who we are and then what we are permitted to do and what are our capabilities.
* Example -> Suppose you login with email and password and then authentication takes place and it identifies that this user is “Linus” then the authorization takes places that what Linus is permitted to do if Linus is admin then he can delete users.
